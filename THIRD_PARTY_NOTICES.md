## When you swap in your own icon set or font

gohud is designed so you can plug in any icon set (`GoConfig.icons`) or theme font.
The license of whatever you plug in is **yours to honour**, not gohud's:

- Commercial icon fonts (for example *Font Awesome Pro*) are usually licensed for use inside
  your product but **not for redistribution** as loose files. Keep them in your game project,
  not inside a fork of gohud that you publish.
- Record where an icon set came from in `GoIconSet.attribution` so your own notices stay
  accurate.
